## General
Please bring a valid ID, your insurance card and any previous reports.

## Cardiology
Bring a list of your current medications and any previous ECG or heart test results.

## Dermatology
Bring a list of products you currently use on your skin.

## General practice
Bring a list of your current medications and any symptoms you want to discuss.

## Important
These are general instructions. If a doctor has given you specific instructions for your visit, follow those instead.
