## Opening days and hours
The clinic is open from Sunday to Thursday. The clinic is closed on Fridays and Saturdays. The first appointment of the day is at 09:00 and the last appointment starts at 15:00.

## Appointment times
Appointments are offered at 09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 14:00 and 15:00 on open days.

## Who and what can be booked
The available specialties are cardiology, dermatology and general practice. Each specialty has one doctor, so only one appointment can be held for a given specialty, date and time.

## Booking window
Appointments can be booked from tomorrow up to 14 days ahead. Appointments cannot be made for today or for a past date.

## Information needed to book
To book through this assistant you need your patient ID, the specialty, the date and the time.
