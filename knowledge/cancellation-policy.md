## Rescheduling policy
An appointment can be moved to another available slot of the same specialty. The new slot follows the same rules as a new booking. A cancelled appointment cannot be rescheduled.

## Cancellation policy
A patient can cancel their own appointment through this assistant. A cancelled slot becomes available to other patients.

## Who can change an appointment
An appointment can only be changed or cancelled by the patient it belongs to, using the patient ID and the appointment ID.
